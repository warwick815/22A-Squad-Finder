# Battlefield atmosphere and season podium

Roles & Roster is removed from the website navigation, page routing and role-selection UI. Saved preferences remain visible on profiles; changes are made in Discord. The Discord roles panel and all assigned roles remain intact. Old #roles links return to Overview.

Current season ELO places 1, 2 and 3 have gold, silver and bronze accents in standings. These honours remain based on ELO even when you sort by wallet or combat stats. Ties share competition places: 1, 1, 3 gives two golds and one bronze.

Staff-confirmed season closure stores podium honours in the season archive. Website profiles display Season N Gold/Silver/Bronze Medal, with placing and season. Existing archives also supply medals, without recalculating from current ratings. Medals do not change ELO or permissions. No live season has been closed by creating this package.

Pages use an original vector battlefield silhouette, a strong dark overlay and slow embers. Scene and targets are decorative. Embers have a pause button and are disabled for reduced-motion preferences. Fewer particles are used on mobile; content panels stay opaque enough for reading. This is an original design, not a Battlefield game asset.
